# 🤖 Mission AI-01 — Give AI a Testable Job

**Track:** AI-native development

## Where am I?

A real project or practice area Connor wants to improve.

## What am I learning?

Turning an idea into a bounded specification instead of asking the agent to 'make it better'.

## What do I do next?

1. Choose one small change you actually want.
2. State **WHAT** you want.
3. State **WHY**.
4. Name at least one thing that **must not change**.
5. State **HOW WE WILL KNOW IT WORKED**.
6. Ask Antigravity to inspect the relevant project and propose a plan before code.
7. Challenge one assumption or ask one verification question about the plan.

## How do I run it?

No implementation is required until the specification and plan are understandable.

## How do I know it worked?

The final request has a goal, constraint and acceptance check, and Connor can explain why each matters.

## Connor decides

Connor chooses the feature and what must remain unchanged.

## Tracker evidence

Do **not** promote capability merely because Antigravity completed the work.

Potential evidence from this mission:

- DECISION / SPECIFICATION: bounded goal and constraints.
- VERIFICATION: meaningful acceptance check.
- EXPLANATION: why the specification is stronger than an open-ended request.

Add one explicit out-of-scope item and one failure case that should be tested.
