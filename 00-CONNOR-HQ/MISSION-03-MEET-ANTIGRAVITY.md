# 🧠 Mission 3 — Meet Antigravity

## Goal

Learn how to use your AI helper properly.

Antigravity is on the **right side of VS Code**.

It can help explain:

- code
- errors
- commands
- ideas
- how projects work

But you should still do the thinking.

---

## Challenge 1

Open Antigravity on the right.

Ask:

    I am learning programming.
    Explain my python-basics project to me simply.
    Give me one small challenge.
    Do not do everything for me.

---

## Challenge 2

Ask it:

    What does "uv run python" mean?

Read the answer.

Try to explain it back to Apa in your own words.

---

## Challenge 3

Ask it:

    What is one thing I could change in hello.py?

Pick one suggestion and try it yourself.

---

## Important rule

If Antigravity tells you to use:

    sudo

ask Apa first.

That command can change the whole computer.

Also ask before running a command if you do not understand what it does.

---

## ✅ Mission complete when

You can show Apa:

- one thing Antigravity taught you
- one thing you changed yourself

⭐ AI Developer Level 1
